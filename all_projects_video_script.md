# 🎙️ All-in-One Portfolio Projects: 5-Minute Video Script & Storyboard

This script is designed for a **5-minute showcase video** highlighting your entire engineering portfolio. It is divided into 7 distinct scenes (approximately 40–50 seconds each) to allow for a professional, clear voiceover pacing (~130 words per minute), smooth transitions, and visual demonstrations of your project functionalities.

---

## 📋 Production Guidelines
* **Voice Style:** Professional, authoritative, clear, and confident (ideal for ElevenLabs profiles like "Marcus" or "Bella").
* **Subtitles:** Burn subtitles directly into the video. Many corporate decision-makers watch videos on mute on platforms like LinkedIn.
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for YouTube/Web or 1:1/4:5 optimized for LinkedIn.
* **Music:** Subtle, tech-focused, ambient background music (low volume).

## 🛠️ Editing & Screen-Recording Checklist
1. **Highlighting:** When recording the screenshots, use a cursor highlighter (like a yellow circle) to guide the viewer's eyes to specific elements (e.g., status badges, PayPal success logs, encrypt keys).
2. **Transitions:** Use simple, modern transitions like cross-fades or slide-lefts. Avoid complex, distracting animations.
3. **Pacing:** Let the screens linger for 1-2 seconds after the narrator finishes speaking to give the viewer time to read the UI labels.
